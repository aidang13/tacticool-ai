## Platform

web

## Users

Retail shoppers on tacticoolgun.com, the Tacti-Cool Gun WooCommerce store. They are everyday gun owners browsing for accessories and gear, and the widget is present on every store page, including product and checkout pages. A shopper opens it mid-browse to ask "what fits my gun," "is this in stock," or "how does an FFL transfer work," and expects a gun-counter answer, not a form.

Dealers and FFL holders are not a confirmed audience (user did not select them).

## Product Purpose

Bam Bam is an AI chat assistant embedded in the store as a floating chat bubble. It helps shoppers find the right holsters, optics, ammo, magazines, and accessories for their specific firearm, use case, or budget, and answers questions about compatibility, specifications, and store policies (shipping, FFL transfers, returns).

Success, as confirmed by the owner:
- A shopper clicks through from a Bam Bam recommendation to a product page with intent to buy.
- Policy and FFL questions get answered in the widget, so fewer reach email or phone support.

## Positioning

Expert gun-counter knowledge. Tacti-Cool Gun's claim is real counter-staff expertise on fit, compatibility, and setup, and Bam Bam extends that expertise to the website at any hour. The widget should read as the store's expert talking, not as a generic shopping bot.

## Operating Context

- The widget is a self-contained script (`/widget.js`) injected into the WooCommerce storefront. It renders a fixed chat bubble bottom-right that opens a 360px panel, and it shares the page with WooCommerce's own theme, navigation, and checkout.
- Product answers come from a live WooCommerce REST search (`/wp-json/wc/v3/products`, in-stock and published only, cached five minutes per query). The assistant sees name, price, stock status, short description, and permalink for up to six matches and is instructed to recommend specific products with price and URL.
- Replies are generated by OpenAI `gpt-4o-mini` with the last ten turns of history. Responses are plain text; links arrive as raw URLs in the text.
- Backend is FastAPI on Python 3.11, deployed via Railway (`railway.toml`, `Procfile`). The widget currently calls `https://tacticool-ai.onrender.com`, so the production API host is on Render, or the two configs are out of sync. Undecided: which host is canonical.
- `sync_catalog.py` can snapshot the catalog to `products.json`, but the app no longer reads that file; `products.json` is an empty array in the repo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities: product search and recommendation, compatibility and spec questions, policy explanation, conversation memory within a session (in-page only, lost on reload).
- Compliance, binding: Bam Bam must not advise on illegal modifications, converting semi-auto to full-auto, or bypassing background checks, and must not give legal advice on state-specific law; it refers shoppers to local regulations. These rules live in the system prompt and apply to any UI copy, suggested prompts, or examples as well.
- No age gate or legal disclaimer is required in the widget (owner did not select it).
- Terminology: "FFL transfer" (Federal Firearms License), "in stock," product categories as named in WooCommerce. The assistant is "Bam Bam"; the store is "Tacti-Cool Gun."
- Input is capped at 400 characters; replies at 600 tokens, with a prompt instruction to keep answers to two to four sentences.
- CORS is open to any origin, so the script can be embedded from the store domain without configuration.
- Undecided: whether policy answers (shipping, returns, FFL steps) should come from a maintained source of truth rather than the model's general knowledge. No policy text exists in the repo today.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and character: "Bam Bam, the Tacti-Cool cave man." The persona is an expert at the gun counter who occasionally drops a subtle cave-man phrase. The owner's rule in the prompt: keep it subtle and fun, not overdone.
- Voice: direct, knowledgeable, no-nonsense, approachable. Concise by default.
- Existing widget copy: header "Talk to Bam Bam," greeting "Ugh! Bam Bam here — your Tacti-Cool cave man. Ask me about holsters, optics, ammo, or any gear!", placeholder "Ask about guns, gear, ammo...", footer "Powered by AI."
- The widget currently uses a near-black and gold scheme and a generic chat-bubble icon. This is incumbent implementation, not a confirmed brand commitment; no logo, mascot artwork, or brand guideline exists in the repo.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live catalog via the WooCommerce API (requires `WOO_URL`, `WOO_CONSUMER_KEY`, `WOO_CONSUMER_SECRET`). Nothing else: no testimonials, reviews, press, customer counts, or performance data in the repo. Future work must not fabricate any of these.
- No mascot illustration or logo file. If Bam Bam gets a visual character, the owner must supply or approve it.
- The storefront itself (tacticoolgun.com) is the authority on real policies and brand; it was not reachable from the environment where this file was written.

## Product Principles

1. Answer like the counter staff would: specific product, price, link, and the reason it fits, in a couple of sentences.
2. Every recommendation should make it easy to get to the product page; the click-through is the point.
3. Never claim stock, price, or fit the catalog does not show. "We don't carry that, look for X" beats a guess.
4. Compliance limits are part of the character, not an apology. Decline cleanly and redirect.
5. The widget is a guest on the store's pages: it must not obstruct checkout, fight the theme, or slow the page.
