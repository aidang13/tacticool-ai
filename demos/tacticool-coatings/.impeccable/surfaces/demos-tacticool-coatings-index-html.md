# Surface: tacticoolcoatings.com home (redesign demo)

Scope: the single marketing page for Tacti-Cool Coatings. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a gun or hard-goods owner near Chickasha, OK, phone in hand, who wants to know if this shop can put a specific finish on a specific item and what it will cost. Action: request a quote. Proof available: none real; the Cerakote H-Series catalog (names, codes) is the one factual reference. Everything else is synthetic and labeled.

Constraints: no invented prices, turnaround, reviews, photos, hours, or address; placeholders stay visibly placeholders. Services confirmed: Cerakote (certified applicator), powder coat, laser engraving; firearm and non-firearm items. Sister business to Tacti-Cool Gun at a separate location.

Chosen direction: The Color Card (user's choice over the dealt Hang Rack). Memorable moment: the chip wall is the quote form's first field; tapping a chip fills "Finish: H-265 Flat Dark Earth" and the quote tag recolors live.

Unresolved: contact details, shipping and FFL handling, whether regulated-marking engraving is offered.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A coatings shop sells one decision: which finish. This page is the Cerakote sample-chip deck laid out on the shop counter, and choosing a chip is the first step of asking for a quote. It refuses the category arrangement of dark hero photo, three service cards, gallery grid, contact form.

OWN-WORLD: Ground is the counter's black rubber mat, charcoal not pure black, with a fine diamond-tread texture. On it sit coated aluminum sample chips: rounded rectangles with a punched hole at the top-left corner, a soft gloss highlight, each carrying a white label strip with its H-code and name in small capitals. Color lives in the chips, 30-60% of every viewport; at least thirty real Cerakote colors in the wall. Type: Barlow Condensed (stamped-plate display, heavy, set large) and Barlow (body), tabular figures for codes. Controls are chips and label strips; rules are hairline galvanized grey; the quote form is a manila hang tag with a brass eyelet. Accents only from chips the visitor picked. No gradients as decoration, no icon tiles.

STORY: Within seconds the visitor sees finishes, not a shop: "this is the place that puts these colors on things." They find their color, see it drop into a quote tag with their item named, and send it. They believe the shop is specific and competent because the page speaks in catalog codes and job language. They leave having sent a quote or holding a code to come back with.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width chip wall filling roughly 60% of the viewport width, 7 to 8 chips per row on desktop, family tabs (Blacks, Earths, Greys, Greens, Reds, Blues, Metallics) across its top as deck tabs. One line above it, large: "Pick the finish. We'll quote the job." with the shop name and the three services as a single line underneath. Right third: the hang tag, cream manila with eyelet, titled "Quote tag," showing Item (select), Service (three check lines), Finish (filled by the chip wall, with a live chip), Accent (optional second chip), and a visible "Send for a quote" button inside the viewport. Nav is a thin strip above everything with the shop name, Finishes, Services, Items, How it works, Find us. Mobile: wall first at 4 chips per row, a sticky bottom bar reading the chosen finish with "Quote this finish," which scrolls to the tag.

FORM: The Color Card, position 1 on the ordered list (the roll assigned position 6, The Hang Rack; the user chose the pick). seed:3564fbad16efdea2. Signature interaction: choosing a chip lifts it with an offset soft shadow, and the quote tag's swatch and label cross-fade to it with an exponential ease-out; one authored motion grammar, no per-section entrances.
