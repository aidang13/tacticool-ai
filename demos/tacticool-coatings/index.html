Three ways to finish it

One shop, three processes. Pick by the part: thin-film Cerakote where fit matters, powder coat where a thicker shell is fine, engraving on top of either.

Property CerakoteCeramic thin-film coating, oven cured Powder coatElectrostatic powder, baked to a shell Laser engravingMarks cut into the surface

How a job goes

  1. 1

    Send the tag

    Item, finish, photos. We reply with a price and a turnaround for that job, not a chart.

  2. 2

    Bring it in

    Drop the item at the shop. Ask us about shipping before you send anything.

  3. 3

    Strip, blast, mask, coat, cure

    Every part comes apart, gets blasted clean, masked where it mates, coated, and baked.

  4. 4

    Pick it up

    Reassembled. We call when it is ready.
