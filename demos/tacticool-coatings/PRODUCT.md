# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated (assumed, not asked): a single static HTML/CSS/JS page with self-hosted fonts, because this is a redesign demo of a marketing site and the repo has no frontend framework. Swap for a CMS theme when the demo is approved.

## Users

Gun owners, hunters, and shooters in and around Chickasha, Oklahoma, plus people with non-firearm hard goods (knives, optics, automotive and motorcycle parts, tools) who want a durable refinish. They arrive with a specific item in mind and a picture of the finish they want, usually from a phone, often from a search or a referral from Tacti-Cool Gun. Their job: find out whether this shop can do that finish on that item, what it costs, and how long it takes.

## Product Purpose

Tacti-Cool Coatings is a refinishing shop. The website exists to turn an interested owner into a quote request: the visitor names the item, the finish, and the colors, attaches photos, and the shop replies with price and turnaround. Success is a submitted quote request with enough detail that the shop can answer it in one reply.

Secondary jobs: make the services and the kinds of items accepted unmistakable, and route gun-store questions to the sister business rather than absorbing them.

## Positioning

A Cerakote certified applicator that also powder coats and laser engraves, under one roof, run by the same people as the Tacti-Cool Gun shop. Confirmed by the owner: Cerakote certified applicator status, laser engraving, powder coat, and non-firearm items are all real offerings. Nothing else has been confirmed; no claim about turnaround, warranty, price, or volume may appear as fact.

## Operating Context

- Sister business to Tacti-Cool Gun (Chickasha, OK) under the same owners, at a separate location with its own address, hours, and contact. Address, hours, phone, and email were not supplied and are marked as placeholders in the demo.
- Customers drop items off in person or ship them; which of these applies, and whether firearms need an FFL for shipped work, is undecided and must not be asserted.
- Services: Cerakote (ceramic coating for firearms and hard goods), powder coat, laser engraving, on firearms and non-firearm items.
- Cerakote colors are a real public catalog with named codes (for example H-146 Graphite Black, H-265 Flat Dark Earth, H-234 Sniper Grey, H-148 Burnt Bronze). The demo may use real Cerakote color names and codes as catalog facts; it must not claim which ones are in stock.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary action: request a quote. The form collects item type, service, color or finish, notes, photos, and contact. Submission is not wired to a backend in the demo.
- Compliance: the site must not offer advice on illegal modifications or legal questions about firearms; coating and engraving are finish work. Any engraving of regulated markings (serial numbers, NFA engraving) is an undecided service and is not advertised.
- Undecided: pricing, turnaround, warranty, shipping and FFL handling, hours, address, phone, email, whether walk-ins are accepted.
- Terminology: "Cerakote," "H-Series" color codes, "powder coat" (one word in Cerakote's own copy varies; the owner wrote "Powdercoat"), "laser engraving," "quote."

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Tacti-Cool Coatings." Family resemblance to Tacti-Cool Gun is expected, but no logo, mark, color, or type was supplied. The Bam Bam cave-man persona belongs to the gun store's chat widget and is not a commitment for this site.
- Voice: direct and knowledgeable, the same counter-staff register as the gun store. No hype.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No gallery photos, no certification badge artwork, no reviews, no pricing, no process photos, no logo. Every image, review, example job, and number in the demo is synthetic, labeled as such on the page, and listed for replacement. The Cerakote color catalog (names and codes) is the one real reference the demo can draw on.

## Product Principles

1. The quote request is the page's job; everything else exists to make that request specific.
2. Show the finish, not the shop: color and material do the persuading.
3. Say only what the owner confirmed; placeholders are visibly placeholders.
4. One voice with the gun store, its own identity as a shop.
5. Phone-first visitors with a photo in hand: the path from photo to quote is short on a small screen.
